Drag a distribution.

The slider's position is a probability and its ticks are values. Step sizes follow the local density: fine where the distribution is concentrated, coarse in the tails. No major or minor ticks, just how much room each tick has.

Every slider here is a qbar slider. A location sits on a normal track as wide as its scale and recentres when you let go; a scale sits on an uncapped exponential track with rate 1 / |location|.
